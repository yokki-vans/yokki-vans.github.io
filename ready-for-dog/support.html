How can we help?

Contact evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com for help with Ready for Dog?. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Please omit private routes, room images and payment details.

Restore Remove Ads

Open Settings → Ads and purchases → Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase and an internet connection. This is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Apple manages payment and refund requests at Report a Problem.

Walks

Start a walk in the app and allow location access. For route recording, enable Precise Location in iOS Settings. The app can record an active walk while the screen is locked. Pause or finish it when you are done. Poor GPS reception may reduce accuracy; elapsed time alone is not proof of distance.

AR care

Allow camera access and move your phone slowly in a well-lit room with visible surfaces. Return to the same room to find a saved bowl or care location. If tracking cannot recover, use the available manual care option or set up the location again. A manual entry is identified as manual.

Play without your phone

Choose a session duration, start the timer and put the phone down. The app keeps the planned end time while backgrounded. It cannot monitor your use of other apps or prove that you avoided the phone.

Family and privacy

Family sharing is optional and uses iCloud. Check that you are signed in and have available iCloud storage. Manage invitations and board access on the Family screen. Local data erasure does not delete a shared board; manage the board first if needed. See our privacy policy for details.

About the rehearsal

Ready for Dog? is a virtual routine rehearsal. Breed profiles and readiness summaries are educational estimates, not veterinary advice or a guarantee that a real dog will behave a particular way. Individual dogs have different needs.
