Family sharing terms

Ready for Dog? · Android · Version 1 · 4 November 2026

Google family sharing is an optional private way to coordinate dog-care plans with people you invite. It is separate from the iOS iCloud service. You can continue using the personal routine without joining a family. Before creating, joining, inviting or publishing family content, the app asks you to accept these terms.

Share respectfully and with permission

Only share care plans, names, assignments and absence notes that you are entitled to share. Obtain permission before including another person's private information. Do not upload passwords, payment details, identity documents, precise addresses or unrelated sensitive information.

Harassment, threats, hate or discriminatory abuse, sexual content, exploitation, illegal material, impersonation, privacy violations and spam are prohibited. Do not use family names, notes or invitations to target or harm another person. Do not try to bypass membership, reporting or blocking controls.

Private groups and local copies

Only invited Google accounts can join a family. Members can publish and explicitly apply shared plan versions. A family code alone does not grant access. A member can leave; an owner can remove a member or delete the shared board. Previously imported local plans may remain on participants' devices. No member should treat the family board as an emergency, medical or veterinary service.

Report a concern

In Settings → Family and help → Google family, open the relevant board and choose Report shared content or Report person. Select a reason, describe the concern and press Send private report. The app confirms only after the report is saved to the service. Your report is private to the app administrator; it is not shown to the reported person or other members. Do not attach unrelated personal details.

The developer, Yevhenii Svistunov, reviews reported concerns and can remove objectionable content or shared access and restrict abusive accounts. You can also contact evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com for support or follow-up. Reporting is not an emergency service; contact the appropriate local service if someone is in immediate danger.

Block a person

Use Block person beside a member. When confirmed online, the app removes existing shared access in every current family with that account: an owner removes that member and any existing invitation for their email, while a non-owner leaves the board. The account is then blocked on this device: its family content is hidden and joining a family containing that account is prevented. If a later membership change creates another common family, the hidden-content placeholder lets you remove that shared access. Local plans already imported remain. Unblocking allows future interaction but does not rejoin a board or restore access. Blocking preferences are per account and device; they do not automatically sync to another device.

Privacy and changes

See the Android privacy policy for what family sharing and safety reports send, who can access them, and retention and deletion. The app stores your acceptance of this terms version locally for your account. A material update will require acceptance of the new version before further family uploads.
