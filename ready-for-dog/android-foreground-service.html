Why a location foreground service is used

Walking is part of the app's virtual dog-care routine rehearsal. After the user explicitly starts a walk, the location foreground service records active time and, when a GPS fix is available, route segments and distance. It continues during that session when the screen is locked or another app is open. Starting late or stopping when the app leaves the foreground would lose the beginning or middle of the user-requested walk.

The service has an ongoing notification with Pause and Finish actions. The user can also pause, resume or finish from Walk. Pausing stops location updates. Finishing stops the service and removes its notification. The recording above demonstrates the in-app controls and background continuation; it does not open the notification shade.

The app does not automatically start walks, request the background-location permission, or use this foreground service for weather polling or passive monitoring. Precise routes stay on the device and are not shared with the family board or advertising. Google family sign-in, camera access and purchases are not required to use Walk.

Reviewer steps

Open the app without signing in, choose a dog, open Walk and tap Start a walk. Allow location and notifications when prompted. Observe the timer, briefly open another screen, then return. Pause, continue or finish using the visible controls. For an outdoor route test, use an open area with a reliable GPS signal.
