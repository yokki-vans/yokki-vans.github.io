Privacy policy

Ready for Dog? · Effective 3 November 2026

Ready for Dog? helps you rehearse a dog-care routine with a virtual companion. This policy explains what the iPhone app processes, what stays on your device, and the optional services it uses. The developer is Yevhenii Svistunov. Contact: evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com.

Your routine and journal

The app stores your dog choice and name, schedule, care history, journal reflections, household plans, budget estimates, preferences and activity progress on your device. The app does not operate a developer account or upload your journal to a developer server. Device or iCloud backups managed by Apple may include app data, according to your backup settings. A report leaves the app only when you choose to share it through the system share sheet.

Walks and location

If you grant location permission and start a walk, the app records the route, distance and timing on your device. Recording may continue in the background during that active walk, including when the screen is locked. Pause or finish the walk to stop recording. You can change permission in iOS Settings. The app does not send your precise route to the developer, advertising providers or family members. The advertising SDK is configured not to access the location granted to the app.

Camera and augmented reality

The camera is used when you open an augmented-reality activity, such as placing a bowl or finding a virtual mess. AR maps and placement anchors are stored locally so the app can recognize the place later. The app does not record camera video, upload camera images or record microphone audio. Standard device backups may include saved AR maps.

Optional family sharing

If you choose to create or join a family board, Apple CloudKit stores and shares the board through your iCloud account. Invited participants can access the dog name and breed, trial dates, task schedule and targets, helper names, assignments and completion records. Your GPS route, AR maps, private diary reflections, budget and absence plans are not included. This is a private invited group, not a public feed. Apple processes account and cloud-service data under its privacy policy.

Use the Family screen to manage access or delete an owned board. Removing local app data does not delete a cloud board or revoke other participants' access. Facts already imported into another participant's journal may remain on their device; each participant controls their own local data.

Advertising and choices

The free app can display Google AdMob banners on the home, journal, walk and play screens. Google may process IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device/app identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party ID and advertising location collection, and does not request cross-app tracking permission. Non-personalized advertising can still require consent for storage and measurement; it does not mean that no data is processed.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. You can reopen available advertising privacy choices in Settings → Remove ads. When consent or an ad is unavailable, the app remains usable without that banner. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps. Ad requests stop when the app is backgrounded. A verified Remove Ads purchase prevents further ad requests from the app; it does not erase information already held by Google.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase. Apple handles payment and account information. The app uses StoreKit to verify the purchase and restore its entitlement; the developer does not receive your card details. Restoring the purchase requires the Apple Account used to buy it. Refunds and revoked purchases are handled according to Apple's transaction status. The local app data deletion action does not cancel or erase an App Store purchase.

Notifications and play sessions

With permission, the app schedules local reminders on your device. You can disable them in iOS Settings. The play timer records its own session and app lifecycle state; it does not inspect other apps, browser activity or overall phone use.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them, erase local data in Settings → Privacy and data, or remove the app and its data. Erasing local data stops the app's active local activities and removes its saved journal, route drafts, AR placements and local family cache. It does not erase copies in Apple backups, shared cloud boards or other people's devices. Manage cloud-board access separately before erasing local data if you want to remove shared information. Apple manages transaction retention; Google manages advertising records under its policies.

Your choices and requests

You can use iOS Settings to control camera, location and notification access, manage iCloud backups, and uninstall the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent or contact a privacy authority. Contact the developer using the address above for help with data the developer controls; contact Apple or Google for account, payment or advertising data held by them. Do not send private GPS routes or room images unless you deliberately choose to include them in a support request.

Children and changes

The app is intended for a general audience considering the responsibilities of dog ownership and is not designed as a child-directed service. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the effective date appears above.
