Delete your account or data

You can request deletion of your Ready for Dog? Android Google family account and associated cloud data without reinstalling the app.

Email an account-deletion request to evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com from the Google email used for family sharing. Include the app name and whether you want your family account, a family board you own, or specified data deleted. Do not send passwords, identity documents, GPS routes or room photos. We may ask you to verify control of the account.

What is removed

After verifying the request, we remove the app's Firebase sign-in account and its membership and invitation records. A family board you own must be deleted before account removal; this also removes its shared plan from the cloud for other members. A non-owner can leave a board without deleting it for everyone. Copies of a shared plan already imported on another member's device may remain on that device.

Delete in the app

Open Settings → Family and help → Google family. Leave or delete your shared boards, then select Delete my family account and confirm. Google may require you to sign in again. This removes the Ready for Dog? Firebase account; it does not delete your Google account.

Local data and purchases

Settings → Privacy and data → Delete local data removes the routine, diary, routes and AR references stored on that device and stops active recording. Uninstalling and removing app data also removes local records. We cannot remotely delete local-only data, exported reports or another person's device copies. Google Play purchases remain associated with your Google account and can be restored; account deletion does not request a refund.

Retention

Google states that deleted Firebase authentication records are removed from live and backup systems within 180 days. Security logs and records that providers must retain follow their applicable policies. See our Android privacy policy for the full data and provider details.
