Android support

Contact evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com. Include the app version, Android version, device model and steps to reproduce the problem. Please omit private routes, room images and payment details.

Purchases

Open Settings → Ads and purchases → Restore purchases using the Google account that made the purchase. Two collars are free, eight are optional cosmetic purchases, and Remove Ads is a separate one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions. Prices and availability are shown by Google Play. Google manages payment and refund requests.

GPS walks

Start recording explicitly from Walk and allow precise location. An ongoing walk uses a visible foreground-service notification and can continue while the screen is locked. Pause or finish it when done. Location accuracy depends on the device and reception. A recovered interrupted draft does not count time after the process stopped.

AR places and fur

AR requires a compatible device, Google Play Services for AR and camera permission. Slowly scan a textured floor in adequate light. To restore a place across sessions, import the original image of a stationary, detailed flat picture in your room, enter its actual width, then recognize it before placement. Keep the reference fixed. Android uses this local image-relative placement, not an iCloud or ARKit room map. A placement without a recognized reference lasts only for the current session.

Move within 1.5 m of a visible object. Tap a tuft and then Collect, or swipe across it. If tracking is unavailable, use the explicitly marked manual record or place the object again. Camera images and references remain on your device.

Brushing and play

Brushing requires 60 seconds of gentle moving contact on the dog's body; a stationary touch or contact outside the body does not count. Completing the session opens a belly-rub reward. In Play, place the phone on a stable surface, screen up or down. While the play screen is visible, movement pauses the timer and about two seconds of stillness resume it. When locked or in another app, a running timer continues and a paused timer stays paused; movement is checked only on the visible play screen.

Weather and family

Weather is optional and uses MET Norway with approximate coordinates rounded to about 3 km. Without a current forecast the room uses time-based lighting. Google family sharing is optional and separate from iOS iCloud families. Only an explicitly shared family plan is uploaded; private routes, room references and journal reflections are not shared.
