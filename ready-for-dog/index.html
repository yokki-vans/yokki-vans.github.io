Ready for Dog?

Try the everyday rhythm of caring for a dog before making the commitment.

Choose a companion from 11 dogs and 37 coats on iPhone or iPad. Build a 14- or 21-day routine with real GPS walks, optional AR care, brushing and time together. Reflect on your records without a readiness score.

Brush your dog to unlock belly rubs, preview ten collar styles and set your device aside for quiet play. Room lighting follows local time, with optional weather from Apple Weather.

Personal practice needs no app account. The app is free with banner ads; ad removal and eight cosmetic collars are separate optional one-time purchases. Two collars are free. There is no subscription.
