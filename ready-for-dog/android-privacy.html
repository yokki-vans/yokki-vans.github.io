Android privacy policy

Ready for Dog? · Android · Effective 3 November 2026

Ready for Dog? helps you rehearse a dog-care routine with a virtual companion. This policy covers the Android app, including optional Google family sharing. The developer is Yevhenii Svistunov. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com. The iPhone and iPad version has a separate policy.

Your routine stays on your device

You can use the personal routine without an account. Dog appearance, name and sex, equipped collar, routine, care history, private journal, household plans, budget estimates, preferences and activity progress are stored in the app's private storage. The app does not send these personal records to a developer server unless you explicitly share the limited family plan described below. Reports leave the app only when you choose to share them through Android.

Android app backup is disabled in the app configuration. Walk archives and AR reference images are also stored in Android's no-backup storage. Manufacturer device-transfer tools and any reports you export are outside the app's control. The app does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics; service providers can still process the technical information described below.

Walks and location

When you grant location permission and start a walk, the app records GPS points, distance, active time and route segments locally. A foreground service with an ongoing notification can continue that active walk while the screen is locked or another app is open. Pause or finish the walk to stop recording. The app does not request always-on background location permission and does not record walks before you start one. Precise routes are not sent to the developer, family boards, weather provider or advertising requests.

Camera and augmented reality

The camera is used while an AR activity is open, for example placing a bowl or collecting virtual fur. The app does not record camera video or microphone audio. To restore a placement across sessions, you can choose a reference image through Android's file picker. Its local copy, physical width and image-relative placement are stored on this device. A placement without a recognized reference is limited to the current AR session. The app does not upload these images or placements, and does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API.

AR features use Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. ARCore collects Google account identifiers when signed in, or device identifiers otherwise, along with AR API usage, app activity, performance and diagnostic data for operation, analytics and improvement; see how Google Play Services for AR handles data. Removing a saved place removes the app's local reference and placement files. Your original image in Photos or another document provider is not deleted.

Optional local weather

Room lighting can follow local time without location access. If you enable weather and grant location permission, the app obtains a foreground location fix and rounds it to a grid of approximately 3 km before requesting a forecast from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway). Requests go directly to api.met.no with those rounded coordinates and an app-identifying user-agent. MET also receives your IP address and states that its request logs can include the requested coordinates. This is not an anonymous request.

The app does not upload your GPS route for weather, save weather coordinates to disk, or share the weather position with family boards or ads. The current rounded position and forecast remain temporarily in memory. Turning weather off or erasing local data clears that state; backgrounding the app stops refresh requests. MET's processing is described in its API terms and privacy policy. Weather visuals interpret forecast data and are not a live view or an emergency-warning service.

Optional Google family sharing

Choosing Google sign-in creates or accesses a Ready for Dog? family account using Firebase Authentication. Google and Firebase process your Google account identifier, Firebase user identifier, email address and profile details returned by sign-in, such as your display name. Authentication also uses technical information including IP address, app identity and user-agent for operation and security. The app does not receive your Google password or access Gmail, contacts or Drive.

Creating a family or pressing the share-plan action uploads a selected snapshot to Cloud Firestore: routine and schedule revisions, trial dates, household member names, task assignments, absence plans including any notes you enter there, and task-completion records. Family membership includes account identifiers, display names, email addresses and roles. An invitation stores the intended recipient's email and an expiry date; the app does not send an email automatically. Invitations expire for joining after seven days, but an unused record can remain until replaced or the board is deleted.

The shared snapshot excludes private journal reflections, raw GPS routes, measured walk or play intervals, AR images and placements, weather location, purchases, budget and personal dog appearance. Confirmations imported from another member do not become activity measured on your device. Only invited, signed-in members can read and update the shared plan through the app. The developer administers the Firebase project and can access stored family records for service operation and support; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage.

The Firestore database is configured in the European eur3 region. Firebase Authentication runs in the United States; other Google service processing follows Google's applicable policies. See Firebase privacy and security information and Google's privacy policy. This Android family service does not sync with the iOS iCloud family board.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free app can show Google AdMob banners on the home, play and walk screens. Google's advertising SDK can process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), advertising and app-set identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not add your dog profile, journal, GPS route, AR references or family records to ad requests. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing and may require consent.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent and privacy choices before advertising requests. Available choices can be reopened from Settings → Ads and purchases → Advertising privacy settings. Android also provides advertising-ID controls. The app remains usable if consent or an ad is unavailable. A verified Remove Ads entitlement stops further banner requests from the app; it does not delete earlier provider records. See how Google uses information from apps and its privacy policy.

Google Play purchases

Where available, Remove Ads and eight optional collars are separate one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchases. Two collars are free; there is no subscription. Google handles payment and account details. The app receives product, purchase-state, purchase-token and signed transaction information to verify access, acknowledge purchases and restore entitlements. The developer does not receive your card details. Use the Google account that made the purchase to restore it. Deleting local data or the family account does not cancel a Google Play purchase. If purchases are not configured or a product is unavailable, the app shows that status and does not start payment.

Motion and notifications

While the play screen is open, accelerometer and gyroscope readings are processed on the device to detect movement. Movement pauses the timer; a short period of stillness resumes it. Session timing is saved, but raw motion samples are not uploaded or retained as a history. A running timer continues while locked; an already paused timer remains paused. The app does not monitor other apps or browser activity.

With notification permission, the app schedules local care reminders and session notifications. These are not Firebase push notifications. You can turn them off in Android Settings. Timing can be affected by Android power-management settings.

Retention, deletion and account removal

Local records remain until you remove them, erase local data from Settings → Privacy and data, or uninstall the app and its data. Local erasure stops the active walk, cancels reminders, removes routine records, route drafts and saved AR references, clears weather state and signs out of the Google family service. It does not delete cloud family boards, other members' local copies, exported reports, Google purchases or service-provider logs.

In Google family sharing, a member can leave a board; its owner can delete the board and its membership and invitation records. Shared schedule entries and confirmations already imported by another person can remain in that person's local plan. To delete your Ready for Dog? family account, first leave or delete all boards, then choose Delete family account on that screen. This deletes the app's Firebase sign-in account, not your Google account. Firebase may require a recent sign-in. Google states that deleted authentication records are removed from live and backup systems within 180 days; security logs and other provider records follow their applicable retention policies.

You can also request family-account or data deletion by emailing evhenii.svistunov@gmail.com from the address used for Google family sharing. Identify Ready for Dog? Android and what you want removed; do not send a password, GPS route or room photo. We may verify account ownership before acting. We cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device or copies controlled by other participants.

Your choices and changes

Use Android Settings to control camera, location and notification permissions, advertising-ID choices and app storage. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent, object to processing or contact a privacy authority. Contact the developer for information controlled by this app; Google and MET handle their own service records under the linked policies.

The app is intended for a general audience considering dog ownership and is not designed as a child-directed service. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer. We may update this policy as features or providers change; the effective date appears above.
